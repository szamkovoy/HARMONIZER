## Decision Log

- **2026-07-04 (12):** Wearable BPM responsiveness + dense-HRV motion-RR gate. Field test `1783116290771` (BLE 10 min, push-ups at 100 s, algoVer 1.2.21) showed two distinct issues. (1) **Pulse plateau at 131 bpm**: the real HR peak during push-ups was ~135 bpm (Polar RR 445 ms), but `measuredPulseRateBpm` rendered a flat 131 plateau for ~7 s. There is no hard cap — the cause is over-smoothing on the trusted path: `pushTrusted` took the median of a 10 s RR window (`PULSE_WINDOW_MS`) and `updateDisplayBpm` then took a 2.5 s median of the candidate-BPM pool. The double median flattened the brief exercise peak and lagged the decline. Fix: trusted sources (wearable/simulated/emulated) now use a shorter `PULSE_WINDOW_TRUSTED_MS = 6 s` window, and `updateDisplayBpm` gains a `directCandidate` mode (used from `pushTrusted`) that sets `displayBpm = candidateBpm` directly when reliable (bypassing the 2.5 s pool median) — the 6 s median is already robust to a single missed beat, so the second smoothing layer is unnecessary for trusted RR. The optical path is unchanged (still 10 s + pool + slew). Effect: the exercise peak shows at its true ~133–135 bpm and the decline tracks without a plateau; the user's "raise to 145" was the perceived ceiling, not a real limit. (2) **RMSSD spike to 56 ms + stress-index V-dip to ~0** during the push-up burst, coinciding with the first RSA gap. Root cause: the Polar H10 emits motion-corrupted RR during movement (R-peak detection errors) — the series jumps `576↔717↔499↔691 ms` (ΔRR ±148–192 ms) while the real rhythm is ~730 ms. These values pass the dense-HRV hard range (300–1500 ms) and are NOT caught by Hampel (they arrive in bursts, not isolated outliers, so the running MAD stays large and the median shifts with them). They inject huge ΔRR → RMSSD spikes to 56 ms, and inflate MxDMn in the Baevsky window → SI → 0 → stress % plunges. Fix: a sequential-deviation gate in `buildDenseHrvSeriesFromBeats` (same rule as the engine's wearable filter: reject RR deviating more than `max(110 ms, median·0.22)` from the running context median of the last 12 accepted RR) applied before Hampel. Simulation on the field export drops the RMSSD peak 56 → ~25 ms (physiologically plausible mild elevation across the rest→exercise transition) and rejects only 13/770 RR — the motion noise — while resting RSA variability (±100 ms ≈ 14%) is preserved; the stress V-dip resolves with it (MxDMn returns to normal). The RSA chart's two gaps during the push-up burst are honest and retained: per-second RSA there is 27–40 bpm (the real HR swing 60→135 over a breath), which exceeds `RSA_RESULTS_OUTLIER_BPM = 20`, so the outlier filter gaps it — RSA is not meaningful during exercise and faking a 40 bpm "RSA" point would mislead. Confirmed no other (bug-caused) RSA gap sources: the only gates are `metricInsideGap` (nonLive) and the 20-cap, and there is no nonLive interval during the on-body push-up period. Existing engine/pipeline/series suites still green (97/97). algoVer → 1.2.22.
- **2026-07-03 (11):** Off-body frozen-RR detection for chest straps. Field tests `1783096820335` (BLE, sensor removed 100→140 s) and `1783098346952` (BLE 10 min, push-ups) showed that a Polar H10 lifted off the chest keeps streaming HR/RR packets with **no real cardiac waveform**: the RR intervals collapse to a near-constant value (field: an exact `659 ms` run → bogus `91 bpm`) and the HR field jumps wildly (`115/120/75/85/140/150/169/91`). The existing on-body filter (`filterOnBodyWearableRrIntervals`, range 350–1500 ms) cannot catch this — 659 ms is in range, so the frozen beats were ingested as real, which produced every BLE artifact the user flagged in this round: (a) the post-gap "step" / ~91 bpm spike on the measurement graph and the synthetic-pulse hold at 91; (b) the RSA fragment + near-vertical drop after the gap (the dense RSA window spanned the frozen beats); (c) the RMSSD chart starting at the Y-axis max (77.3 ms) after the gap and smoothly declining, plus the late needle spike — all from ΔRR across the frozen/off-body beats. The Polar H10 does not set the standard HRP "sensor contact supported" bit, so `sensorContactDetected` is `null` and cannot gate this. Fix: a pure helper `isFrozenRrRun(recentRrMs, minCount=6, toleranceMs=8)` in `wearableRrQuality.ts` — real RR always vary beat-to-beat (≥10 ms even at very low HRV), so a run of 6 consecutive RR within ±8 ms of their mean is physiologically impossible and is a reliable off-body signature. `BleHeartRateSource.ingestRrIntervals` now accumulates plausible RR into a rolling `recentRrMs` (last 24); once a frozen run is detected it stops ingesting beats, latches `signalLost` (so the pipeline falls through to emulated synthetic pacing instead of recording fake beats), and clears the history; when real variability returns the latch clears and the timeline resets so frozen beats don't seed the post-return timeline. Disconnect also clears the history/latch. Effect: an off-body strap now reads as signal loss (gray + synthetic pacing) instead of a frozen bogus HR — eliminating the 91 spike and the downstream RMSSD/RSA gap artifacts at the source. The pre-gap "V-dips" on RSA are honest low-HRV beats (not artifacts). Covered by `wearableRrQuality.test.ts` (4 new cases). algoVer → 1.2.21. Paired with: results-chart header window, coherence auto-scale, and the pulse-source persistence fix (see practices/breath histories).

- **2026-07-03 (10):** Finger-off bridge cap. Field test `1783095121380` (algoVer 1.2.19, same 3×3 s + 1×20 s lift protocol) showed a residual plateau artifact: the 20 s finger lift drew a perfectly flat ~67.1 bpm "live" plateau for ~12 s on the measurement graph before the gray bar started — the 1.2.19 bridge anchoring (candidate = `60_000/stableRr`) made the held baseline visually flat, and the 12 s bridge budget (1.2.19) meant a deliberate 20 s finger-off was masked as 12 s of fake "live" pulse. The user flagged this as a synthetic-pulse plateau that should have been marked as a gap. The 3 s lifts in the same session re-locked in 3–6 s, so the 12 s budget was only ever consumed by the long deliberate lift. Root cause: the bridge budget is finger-state-agnostic — it cannot tell a deliberate finger-off (where the user knows there is no pulse and expects gray) from a marginal-PPG re-lock with the finger still on (where interpolation is the whole point). Fix: new `OPTICAL_FINGER_OFF_BRIDGE_CAP_MS = 4 s` and a pure helper `applyFingerOffBridgeCap({snapshot, contactPresent, nowMs, capMs})` applied in `pushOpticalSample` after the bridge override — when `!contactPresent` and the gap since the last real beat exceeds the cap, the bridge is cut to gray (`bridgingShortGap=false, reacquiring=true, bpm=0`). While the finger is present the full 12 s re-lock budget is preserved (marginal-PPG re-lock stays bridged). Effect: a 3 s test lift (finger off ≤3 s) stays bridged; a 20 s lift shows a ~4 s bridge plateau then gray (instead of a 12 s live plateau); a marginal-PPG re-lock with the finger on stays bridged up to 12 s. The post-gap first point (~72.4 bpm, real HR after the 20 s off, settling to 70) is honest — the visual "spike" is the jump from the stale bridge baseline to the real post-gap HR, not a miscalculation. Covered by `biofeedback-pipeline.test.ts` "applyFingerOffBridgeCap" (4 cases). algoVer → 1.2.20.

- **2026-07-03 (9):** Bridge-candidate anchoring + 12 s bridge budget. Field test `1783093877906` (algoVer 1.2.18, same 3×3 s + 1×20 s lift protocol) showed two residual artifacts: (1) a sharp **post-gap edge spike** to ~84.7 bpm right after the 130 s lift (pre-gap ~71.6), with a second smaller spike nearby — the user flagged both as RR-miscalculation artifacts at gap boundaries; (2) a **wide gray band** after the 160 s lift where interpolation did NOT fire (unlike the 100/130 s lifts). Root cause of (1): `bridgingShortGap` stays true while the gap is in the 10 s window, but `candidateBpm` was computed from the **mixed** recon median (synthetic baseline beats + real post-gap beats). The first 1–2 s of post-gap beats carry artifactualy short RR (zero-phase bandpass ring on the `dropSamplesSince` discontinuity + motion from finger return), and as they accumulated they pulled the mixed median up to ~84 — exactly the "edge spike at gray-band boundary" the user described, even though the bridge is supposed to HOLD the baseline. The RR-drift gate is disabled during bridging (`!bridging`), so it could not catch it. Root cause of (2): the 160 s lift's re-lock took ~15 s on marginal post-return PPG (the 1.2.18 relax-sweep re-opened the detector but the pulse oscillations were genuinely below the bandpassed noise floor), exceeding the 8 s bridge budget and leaving ~7.6 s gray. Fix: (1) in `PulseBpmEngine.pushOptical`, during `bridging` the `candidateBpm` is anchored to `60_000/stableRr` (the baseline) instead of the mixed median — the bridge now actually holds the last stable rhythm while the gap is in the window; once the gap ages out, `bridging` clears and the (now settled) real beats drive BPM again. The drift gate still guards against persistent garbage rhythm outside bridge. (2) `OPTICAL_INTERP_MAX_MS` and `OPTICAL_OPEN_BRIDGE_MAX_MS` raised 8 s → 12 s, covering a 3 s lift + up to ~9 s re-lock (the 160 s case → ~3 s gray tail instead of 7.6 s); genuine 20 s losses still far exceed the budget and fall through to reacquire → emulated. Covered by `pulse-bpm-engine.reacquire.test.ts` "anchors BPM to baseline during a bridged gap" and updated 20 s-loss / hard-reacquire tests. algoVer → 1.2.19.

- **2026-07-03 (8):** Peak-detector re-acquire sweep. Field test `1783091581263` (algoVer 1.2.17) still showed a ~24 s start-of-session gray band: the peak detector lost lock in settle's tail (last beat ~11 s before `running`) and didn't re-lock until t≈13 s on marginal PPG (cold finger / weak perfusion). `detectBeats` is stateless and its thresholds are adaptive-low (MIN_ACCEPTED_PEAK_VALUE = 0.0004), so the loss is mostly genuine signal absence — but the 35th-percentile-of-local-maxima term in `heightThreshold`/`prominenceThreshold` can float with a sparse noise floor and block marginal pulse peaks that ARE present, extending the re-lock window. Fix: `detectBeats` gains a `relaxThresholds` param; `BiofeedbackPipeline.pushOpticalSample` passes `relaxThresholds = (now - lastMergedBeat) >= OPTICAL_REACQUIRE_RELAX_MS (2.5 s)` when contact is present. In relax mode the percentile term is dropped and the robustScale fractions halved (0.22→0.11 height, 0.18→0.09 prominence), re-opening the detector to sub-threshold pulse oscillations. Downstream `filterPulseRrMeasurements` and the engine's coherence gate still reject erratic noise beats, so the sweep cannot seed bogus pacing — it only re-opens the door to real pulse that was just below the nominal threshold. The deep root (peak detector losing lock on marginal PPG at all) is mitigated, not eliminated: if the pulse oscillations are genuinely below the bandpassed noise floor, no threshold tuning recovers them and the practice correctly falls through to the start-grace emulated fallback (practices history 2026-07-03 (9)). Covered by `peak-detector.test.ts` (2 cases). algoVer → 1.2.18.

- **2026-07-03 (7):** Plausible-baseline bridge + sanitized camera-hold/emulated seeds. Field test `1783091581263` (algoVer 1.2.16, same protocol) still showed many **spontaneous** short gray bands (t≈17.7, 31, 47.6, 56.6, 241–249 s — age 0.9–2.1 s, amplitude 0.008–0.053, i.e. signal was present) not from finger lifts, plus a bogus guidance-graph "floor" at ~44.9 bpm at t=0. Root cause: on marginal PPG the coherence gate (`jitter ≤ max(110, 0.2×RR)`) almost never passes, so `lastStableRrMs`/`lastStableBeatTs` stay 0 for the whole session; consequently neither the engine's empty-window rescue (2026-07-03 (5)) nor the 1.2.16 safety net (2026-07-03 (6), which anchored on the coherent baseline) could fire, and every 1–3 s flicker painted gray. Separately, the camera-hold guidance value fell through its `holdStableBpm` term (0, no baseline) to the noisy `snapshot.pulseRateBpm ≈ 44.9`, producing the start "floor". Fix: (1) new pipeline field `lastPlausibleBpm` = the last slew-limited `displayBpm` in 50–110 (noise ~46 bpm is excluded by the range, so this is a safe looser baseline), updated every `pushOptical` from the pre-override engine bpm, preserved across finger-off hard-resets (saved/restored alongside `lastStableRrMs`/`lastStableBeatTs`), and exposed via `getLastPlausibleBpm()`. (2) `computePipelineBridgeOverride` signature changed to `bridgeRrMs`/`bridgeAnchorTs` (caller prefers `lastStableRrMs`/`lastStableBeatTs`, falls back to `lastPlausibleBpm`/its ts); the `gapSinceTrusted > gapThresh` gate is replaced by a `snapshot.bpm <= 0` discriminator (only override when the engine actually dropped out, never touch live frames producing a usable bpm); the bridge RR is clamped to 500–1200 ms (50–120 bpm). (3) `CoherenceBreathScreen` now uses `getLastPlausibleBpm()` as the second-tier fallback in three start-of-session paths: the camera-hold guidance value (above the 65 default, eliminating the 44.9 floor), the planner seed at `running`, and `switchToEmulatedPulse`'s camera seed. The ~24 s start gray band is honest — the peak detector lost lock ~11 s before `running` started (warmup tail on marginal PPG, adaptive-prominence stuck, tracked in `open_questions.md`) and the 8 s bridge budget cannot cover a 24 s loss; the emulated ramp that follows now seeds at the plausible ~80 bpm instead of 65. Tests: `computePipelineBridgeOverride` suite expanded to 8 cases (added plausible-fallback bridge + RR clamp). algoVer → 1.2.17.

- **2026-07-03 (6):** Pipeline-level short-gap bridge safety net. Field test `1783089776509` (algoVer 1.2.15, same 3×3 s + 1×20 s lift protocol) showed two short **spontaneous** gray bands not caused by finger lifts — at t≈14–16 s (age up to 1.86 s) and t≈72–73 s (age 1.17 s, amplitude 0.022 — decent) — plus the expected 20 s-lift tail. The engine bridge did not fire on these: on marginal PPG the RR filter's ±16 % deviation gate over-rejects the erratic residual beats, `reconstructOpticalBeatWindow` gets an empty/short `filteredBeatTimestampsMs` (or `openGap < gapThresh` because a recent noisy beat is the filtered tail), so `bridgingShortGap = false`, `lockState = holding`, and `cameraReadyForLog` (which requires `tracking || bridging || looksCoherent`) falls to false → `measuredPulseRateBpm = 0` → gray. The coherent baseline (`lastStableRrMs`) was known in both cases. Fix: a pure pipeline helper `computePipelineBridgeOverride({snapshot, nowMs, lastStableRrMs, lastStableBeatTs, isEmulated, isWearable})` — when the engine did NOT bridge and is NOT reacquiring, but the gap since the last trusted beat is within `OPTICAL_OPEN_BRIDGE_MAX_MS` (8 s) and a coherent baseline exists, it returns a bridge snapshot (`bridgingShortGap = true`, `bpm = 60_000/lastStableRrMs`, `medianRrMs = lastStableRrMs`); applied in `pushOptical` after the engine snapshot. Long gaps (>8 s) and genuinely live frames (gap since trusted beat below threshold) are untouched, and wearable/emulated sources are excluded. The engine-level bridge (2026-07-03 (5) empty-window rescue) and this safety net are complementary: the engine handles empty-window synthesis, the pipeline catches the cases where the engine window still has noisy beats that block the open-tail bridge. Covered by `biofeedback-pipeline.test.ts` "computePipelineBridgeOverride" (6 cases). The start-of-session 85.3→80 bpm ramp is left as-is: it is a smooth ~5 bpm decline over ~5 s on marginal PPG (amplitude 0.007), consistent with warmup HR settling / bandpass settling, not a single-frame spike — suppressing it would require a start gray band, which the user finds worse. algoVer → 1.2.16.

- **2026-07-03 (5):** Empty-window bridge rescue on the coherent baseline. Field test `1783088279299` (algoVer 1.2.14) showed a ~22 s start-of-session gray band: the peak detector lost lock on marginal PPG (amplitude 0.005–0.014) at ~t=9 s and the RR filter's ±16 % deviation gate then over-rejected the erratic residual beats, so `PulseBpmEngine.pushOptical` received an empty `filteredBeatTimestampsMs` window. With `lastBeat == null` the open-tail bridge in `reconstructOpticalBeatWindow` was skipped entirely (`if (lastBeat != null && stableRrMs > 0)`), so even the first 1.8 s of the loss painted a gray band — although a coherent baseline RR (`lastStableRrMs ≈ 789 ms`, 76 bpm) was already known from the brief live prefix. Fix: (1) `BiofeedbackPipeline` now records `lastStableBeatTs` (the source timestamp of the last `looksCoherent` window) alongside `lastStableRrMs` and preserves it across finger-off hard-resets; both are passed to the engine via two new `PulseBpmInput` fields (`pipelineStableRrMs`, `lastTrustedBeatTs`). (2) `pushOptical`'s `stableRr` fallback chain is now `engine.lastStableMedianRrMs → realMedianRr → pipelineStableRrMs`, so the bridge can synthesize on a known-good RR even when both engine-local sources are 0. (3) `reconstructOpticalBeatWindow` gained an empty-window rescue: when `acceptedBeats` is empty but `stableRrMs > 0` and `lastTrustedBeatTs` is within `OPTICAL_OPEN_BRIDGE_MAX_MS`, it synthesizes the window on `stableRr` from that anchor and sets `bridging = true`. Effect: a short loss (≤ 8 s) now stays live and smooth (no gray band, BPM ≈ baseline) regardless of whether the RR filter kept any beats; only losses beyond the 8 s budget fall through to reacquire/emulated, which is the honest representation. The deep root — peak detector not re-locking on marginal PPG for 10–20 s — remains in `open_questions.md`; this change stops those slow reacquisitions from producing visible short-gap gray bands. Covered by `pulse-bpm-engine.field.test.ts` "empty-window rescue". algoVer → 1.2.15.

- **2026-07-03 (4):** Preserve `lastStableRrMs` across finger-off hard-reset. Field test `1783086748164` (algoVer 1.2.13) showed the 20 s finger-lift emulated fallback seeded at 65 bpm (default) instead of the user's real ~80, because the contact detector's `shouldHardReset → softReset()` (fired on long finger absence, both the ultra-fast no-finger path and the main optical path) clears `lastStableRrMs = 0` along with the optical/calibration buffers. `lastStableRrMs` is the coherent baseline used to seed the synthetic sine wave; wiping it on finger-off is counterproductive — the whole point of the baseline is to remember the user's true rate through loss. Fix: save/restore `lastStableRrMs` around the two hard-reset `softReset()` calls in `pushOptical`; the session-start `softReset()` (from `beginFromIdle`) still clears it, so a previous session's baseline cannot leak into a fresh one. Paired with practices history 2026-07-03 (5) which makes the planner seed and camera-hold guidance prefer this baseline over warmup noise.

- **2026-07-03 (3):** Coherent-only stable baseline RR. `BiofeedbackPipeline` now updates `lastStableRrMs` (exposed via `getLastStableRrMs()`, used by the breath screen to seed emulated fallback) only when `bpmSnap.looksCoherent` is true, not on any `trackingNow`. On marginal-PPG sessions the peak detector can flicker `tracking` on noise and produce erratic medians (~46 bpm from noise); recording those would seed the synthetic sine wave with a bogus rate when the practice later falls back. The `lockState` itself still uses the loose `liveWindowUsableForTracking` gate (anti-freeze design preserved) — only the stable-baseline recording now requires coherence. See practices history 2026-07-03 (4) for the paired breath-screen trust-gate change.

- **2026-07-03 (2):** Optical look-ahead reconstruction: interpolated beat window + full flush on finger return. Field test `1783079328709` (algoVer 1.2.10; 3 s lifts at 100/130/160 s, 20 s lift at 200 s) still showed all four reported artifact classes: a short start-of-session dropout band, 5–10 s `holding` bands after 3 s lifts, 10–15 s re-lock, and BPM spikes at gray-band edges. The optical path was rebuilt around the product constraint that camera pulse is guidance-only and may lag a few beats. (1) **Reconstructed window (`reconstructOpticalBeatWindow`, `pulse-bpm-engine.ts`):** for `fingerCamera` the engine now rebuilds its 10 s window from real accepted beats, filling any gap — closed mid-window or open at the tail — with synthetic beats on the last stable RR when the gap fits the fixed `OPTICAL_INTERP_MAX_MS = 8 s` budget (3 s lift + 2–3 s optical re-lock). Such frames report the new `bridgingShortGap = true` snapshot flag and stay live; straddle-RR and the first artifactual post-gap RR никогда не попадают в медиану, что убирает краевые пики. The budget is a fixed time constant, not a beats count: RR-scaled ceilings made the same gap flip between "interpolable" and "hard" as the post-gap rhythm drifted (seen as 0.3–1.5 s live-flicker bands in replay). Gaps beyond the budget go to `reacquiring` (hold BPM, wait for `POST_GAP_MIN_RR = 5` clean real RR); a settled hard gap no longer blocks bridging of later short gaps. Synthetic beats never leave the engine (`filteredBeatTimestampsMs` is real-beats-only). (2) **Sequential RR-filter context reset:** `filterPulseRrMeasurements` resets its median context across a gap `> 2×PULSE_RR_MAX_MS`. Previously post-gap RR were validated against the PRE-gap median; if the rhythm shifted during the loss (e.g. +15 bpm), every new RR failed the ±16 % deviation gate, rejected RR never updated the context, and the entire post-gap signal was discarded until pre-gap history aged out of the 2-min window — this deadlock was the dominant cause of the 10–15 s reacquisition. (3) **Full optical flush on real finger return (`biofeedback-pipeline.ts`):** reverted 2026-07-02 (8) `dropSamplesSince` back to `optical.reset()` on regain after `≥ CONTACT_REGAIN_FLUSH_MS`. Partial drops leave a multi-second time hole in the 12 s window; the zero-phase bandpass rings across that discontinuity for as long as pre-lift samples remain (up to ~12 s), which — not the refill cost — was the real re-lock bottleneck. With a clean flush the bandpass runs on contiguous samples (bypass under 16 samples, then matched SOS) and yields usable peaks in ~2–3 s, while the engine's open-gap bridge hides the refill from the practice. (4) **Optical display slew limit:** published camera `displayBpm` is clamped to 6 bpm/s (wearable path untouched) so pool-composition steps at bridge boundaries cannot jump the guidance BPM. (5) **Liveness threading:** `pulseLog` entries carry `bridgingShortGap`; `CoherenceBreathScreen` camera-live gate and `isPulseLogEntryLiveForMeasurement` treat bridged frames as live regardless of real-beat age, so bridged gaps draw no gray band. Verified by `pulse-bpm-engine.field.test.ts` (3 s lift, open-gap re-lock, edge-spike, 20 s loss, start-of-session hiccup scenarios) and a replay of export `1783079328709`: gray bands only for the 20 s lift, zero >8 bpm frame-to-frame spikes. algoVer → 1.2.11.

- **2026-07-03 (1):** Dynamic short-gap interpolation + sustained camera recovery probes. Field test `1783032655745` (algoVer 1.2.9) confirmed that widening the breath-runtime live window to 5 s fixed one of the three 3 s finger lifts, but two lifts still collapsed into another ~5–8 s of `holding + 0 bpm` after the finger had already returned, and the 20 s lift never made it back from emulated fallback before the session ended. Root causes were again on the source side. (1) **Over-eager hard reacquire for short optical gaps:** `PulseBpmEngine` treated *any* beat gap `> 2.5 s` as a post-gap reset and threw away the pre-gap RR context until 5 new RR accumulated. In the field log the first real post-return beat often landed ~3.5–4.0 s after the last pre-gap beat; at ~66 bpm that is still only a few missed beats and is exactly the regime where interpolation should win. The hard 2.5 s rule therefore manufactured a second gray band after the finger was already back. Fix: keep the 5-RR hard reacquire gate only for **genuinely long** gaps; the gap must now exceed a dynamic interpolation ceiling derived from the last stable RR (roughly up to `4–5` missed beats, clamped to `~2.8–5.0 s`) before the engine discards pre-gap RR context. Shorter gaps stay on the existing display/hold path and naturally slide back onto real beats without a fake “new acquisition”. Covered by a new `pulse-bpm-engine.reacquire.test.ts` case. (2) **Camera fallback probes were too short to recover after long loss:** once guidance had fallen to `emulated`, the camera retry loop only unpaused optical for `2.5 s` every `6 s`. That is safe for avoiding false restores, but in the field it starved real recovery: the user put the finger back on the lens, optical fps/signal returned, yet each probe was cut off before enough clean beats accumulated. Fix: retry sooner and let a recovery probe stay active continuously while the optical path is clearly seeing finger/signal signs (finger detected, sufficient SQ/presence, or live fps/amplitude), up to a capped continuous search window, instead of chopping recovery into isolated bursts. The product effect is now aligned with the intended design: short pulse losses are bridged by delayed interpolation; long losses fall to synthetic pacing, but real camera recovery is allowed to keep searching long enough to actually come back. algoVer → 1.2.10.

- **2026-07-02 (14):** Short camera hold/interpolation restored at the breath-runtime boundary. Field test `1783030499402` (algoVer 1.2.8) showed that the low-fps bandpass + honest emulated-status fixes were in place, but brief 3 s finger lifts still rendered as ~8–10 s gray bands. The optical side was no longer the only culprit: the runtime had a semantic mismatch between pipeline lock state and what the breath screen/log called a “live measurement”. `BiofeedbackPipeline` could legitimately stay in `holding`/`tracking` with `event.bpm > 0` and a recent last beat while the camera was bridging a short gap, but `CoherenceBreathScreen` still required `event.hasFreshBeat` plus the old `BREATH_CAMERA_LIVE_BEAT_MAX_AGE_MS = 2.5 s`, so it zeroed `measuredPulseRateBpm` and marked `pulseReady=false` long before the runtime had really exhausted interpolation. That produced fake wide gray zones and paradoxical `tracking + 0 bpm` exports. Fix: raised `BREATH_CAMERA_LIVE_BEAT_MAX_AGE_MS` to **5 s** and, for camera mode, treat any non-emulated sample with `event.lockState !== "searching"`, `event.bpm > 0`, `event.reacquiring !== true`, and beat age within that 5 s window as a live measured pulse. This explicitly codifies the product behavior “ride through ~2–3 beats / a short finger lift by interpolation/hold before declaring real loss.” Truly stale camera data (`> 5 s`) still falls out of live measurement and then into emulated fallback. The same pass also made `pulseLog` honest during emulated camera fallback: while `useEmulatedPulseMode` is active, it now records `pulseSource="emulated"` and `pulseLockState="searching"` instead of transient internal probe states. Covered by `breath-results-series.test.ts` (4 s camera hold stays live; 6 s becomes non-live). algoVer → 1.2.9.

- **2026-07-02 (13):** Low-fps post-gap recovery + honest emulated snapshot state. Field test `1783029200609` (algoVer 1.2.7, camera finger lifts at 100/130/160/200 s) showed that the prior spike/plateau fixes held, but recovery after finger return was still taking ~10 s and the runtime sometimes displayed `tracking`/`0 bpm` or even briefly `75 bpm` before the real optical pulse had returned. Root causes were two independent edge cases.
